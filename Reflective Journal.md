Xinyu's Entry:

I gained insight into how to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) to uncover some patterns within the dataset before jumping to a model. By making a plot instead of just looking at a table of numbers, I can see what’s going on within the data much more clearly than before. The Titanic dataset explores how to compare different aspects of the passengers and how they relate to the results of the passengers who survived the shipwreck.

For the final part of the lab, I looked into the correlation between the port that a passenger embarked from and their survival in the disaster. I used a countplot to make a graph comparing the number of people who survived and did not survive at each port. The results of the graph showed that the ports of Cherbourg, Queenstown, and Southampton had very different results for passengers who survived the disaster. It appears that a higher percentage of passengers who embarked from Cherbourg survived the tragedy than the passengers who embarked from the other two ports.

A relationship in a graph does not necessarily mean that one variable directly influences the other variable. For example, the port of embarkation may affect survival rate in part because of other factors, such as class, that are associated with the embarkation port. Therefore, we need to examine several relevant variables in the data before concluding.
